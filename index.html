Our Ancestral Roots & Clan Identity

The Kintu lineage draws its strength from centuries of cultural heritage in Buganda, anchored by sacred totems, deep respect for elders, and devotion to community advancement.

🐾

Omuziro (Totem)

Olugave (The Pangolin)

Symbolizes resilience, wisdom, and protection. Secondary totem (*Akabbiro*): Akanfuka.

📍

Ancestral Seat (Obukomera)

Katende, Mpigi, Uganda

The founding homestead established in 1898, home to the original family farms and historical burial grounds.

🤝

Traditional Clan Salutation

"Gyebale ko, Baana ba Kintu!"

The warm cultural greeting shared between members to acknowledge shared lineage and royal blessing.

The Four Pillars of Kintu Heritage

1. Obumu

Unbreakable Unity

No branch falls from the tree that remains connected to its roots. Mutual aid across generations.

2. Obuntubulamu

Honor & Modesty

Character is our greatest inheritance. We walk with humility, dignity, and fair play in all matters.

3. Okusoma

Pursuit of Knowledge

Pioneering in academics, science, craftsmanship, and leadership from primary schools to global halls.

4. Okujjukira

Ancestral Reverence

Honoring the sacrifices of those who preceded us and preserving their oral histories for newborns.
