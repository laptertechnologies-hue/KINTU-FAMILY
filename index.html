Ancestral Roots in Bunyoro-Kitara

The Kintu family draws its identity from the ancient kingdom of Bunyoro-Kitara. Our heritage is rooted in the royal traditions, Empaako praise names, communal solidarity, and love for knowledge.

Empaako Tradition

Honorific Praise Names

The sacred Bunyoro social custom: Abwooli, Amooti, Akiiki, Apuuli, Araali, Atwoki, Adyeri, Bbala, Acaali, Atenyi.

Ancestral Homeland

Bunyoro-Kitara, Western Uganda

The historical heartland where our earliest forebears established homesteads, fertile farms, and community foundations.

Traditional Salutation

"Oraire ota, Baana ba Kintu!"

Warm greeting exchanged between descendants to affirm shared bloodline, respect, and mutual kinship.

The Four Pillars of Kintu Family Heritage

1. Obumu

Unbreakable Unity

Strength lies in kinship and shared roots. We support and uplift each other across all borders.

2. Engeso Nungi

Integrity & Character

Preserving the esteemed moral character of Bunyoro-Kitara with humility, honesty, and honor.

3. Okusoma

Wisdom & Diligence

Dedication to education, skilled craftsmanship, entrepreneurship, and leadership.

4. Okwijuka Abakuru

Ancestral Reverence

Honoring the sacrifices of our elders, keeping our stories alive, and passing values to our youth.
